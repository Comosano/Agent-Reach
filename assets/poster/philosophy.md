# Radial Reach

Radial Reach is an aesthetic of a single origin and many destinations. A lone point of warm light sits low on a dark field, and everything else is derived from it: rays, arcs, intervals. Nothing is placed by feel; every mark answers to an angle or a radius, the way a surveyor's plate answers to its instrument.

The palette is near-black ink and pale paper with one amber accent, spent only on the origin and the nodes where each ray lands. Restraint is the content. Hairlines at differing opacities build depth the way graphite builds it, through patient layering rather than fill.

Spacing is metronomic. Fifteen rays divide the arc into equal steps; concentric measure-rings repeat at fixed intervals with small tick marks, a quiet ruler for distance. The repetition is deliberate and meticulously calibrated, the product of countless hours of careful alignment.

Text is a whisper: a title set in a light grotesque, labels in a monospaced hand running along each ray like annotations on a chart. Margins are generous and nothing overlaps. The composition should read as a master-level instrument drawing: precise, calm, and unmistakably crafted.
